🍣 One Ethernet frame. One slot per device. Everybody’s order is already on the plate.
⚡ No stopping. No waiting. No re-packing. The device swaps its slot while the plate rolls by.
🔄 The last device sees nobody after it and sends the plate back (the cable has two lanes).
🔌 Every device needs a special chip (an EtherCAT slave controller).
✂️ Cut the belt here and everyone behind the cut goes hungry. (Ring topology fixes that.)
✂️

Per cycle, 4 devices:

  • PROFINET RT   🏍️🏍️🏍️🏍️ ➜ ➜ 🏍️🏍️🏍️🏍️   = 8 frames
  • EtherCAT        🍣  = 1 frame (it just comes back full)
No store-and-forward, no switch queues: about a microsecond per device. Cycles well under 1 ms are routine.

Verdict: EtherCAT

  • ✅ Crazy fast and very precise timing
  • ✅ Scales to hundreds of devices on one plate
  • ✅ Perfect for many axes in tight sync
  • ⚠️ Special hardware in every device
  • ⚠️ Line/ring wiring, no ordinary switches in the chain
