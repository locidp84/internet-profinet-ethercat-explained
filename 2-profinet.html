Verdict: PROFINET RT

  • ✅ Fast: no IP, no TCP, no handshakes, no receipts
  • ✅ Ordinary Ethernet cables and switches
  • ✅ Right size for most machines
  • ⚠️ One frame per device, in each direction
  • ⚠️ Switch queues add a little jitter
