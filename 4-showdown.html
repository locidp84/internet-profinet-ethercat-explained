🧑‍💼TCP/IP — device web pages, firmware updates, OPC UA, SCADA, cloud. Anything that can wait a moment.
🏍️PROFINET — everyday machine I/O: PLC ↔ drives, valves, I/O blocks, on ordinary switches.
🍣EtherCAT — many axes in tight sync: motion control, robots, fast machines.
They are not enemies: on a real machine they often share one cable. (Your PROFINET drive still has a TCP/IP web page.)
